# TicTacToe (C++)
A terminal-based game of tic-tac-toe written in C++.

## Features
- Play tic-tac-toe in your terminal.
- Two players play as X or O.

## Requirements
- C++ compiler (Makefile defaults to 'g++')
- 'Make'
- Your system's terminal

## Build & Run
The makefile takes care of the build.
In your terminal enter...

'make tictactoe'

*To build without running, enter in your terminal...

'make tictac'

To clean build artifacts, enter in your terminal...

'make clean'

## How to Play
- Board positions are numbered 1-9
- Players choose their marker (X or O)
- Players take turns until there are no more empty spots on the board or a player wins
